# CONSENTUS X-LUCIS v2.0 - Operational Manual

## 1. Introduction
Consentus X-Lucis is a high-fidelity audio engineering suite designed for meditative soundscape production and frequency alignment. Leveraging advanced DSP (Digital Signal Processing), it allows for multi-source layering and real-time mastering of audio streams.

## 2. Core Operation
### The 432Hz Standard (CRITICAL)
For the most effective meditative experience, always ensure the **432Hz Alignment** module is active.
- **Location:** In the **DSP Modules** panel (right sidebar).
- **Function:** When enabled, the engine applies a precise pitch shift to all active audio sources (MP3s, Solfeggio, Environments) to align with the 432Hz "Miracle Frequency".
- **Verification:** Look for the "432Hz" toggle. When blue, the system is actively compensating and aligning all harmonics.

### Multi-Source Layering
1. **File Transport:** Load any MP3 or audio file from your device.
2. **Zen Modulator:** Select from environmental textures (Ocean, Rain, Drone) or Earth's resonance (Schumann).
3. **Solfeggio Frequencies:** Add pure sine-wave tones targeting specific spiritual/physical nodes.
4. **Mastering:** Use the **10-Band Precision EQ** to shape the final output.

## 3. Spectrum Analysis & Diagnostics
The Spectrum Analyzer provides real-time feedback on your audio output:
- **Peak Frequency:** Identifies the dominant tone in your mix. Perfect for verifying Schumann Resonance (approx. 7.83Hz).
- **Musical Note:** Translates the peak frequency into a Western musical scale equivalent.
- **Power Level:** Monitors the total energy output of the system.
- **Band Distribution:** Shows energy levels across the Sub, Bass, Mids, and Highs.
- **Expand View:** Use the "Maximize" icon on the Spectrum panel to open a high-resolution analysis window with full diagnostics.

## 4. Session Capture
1. Set up your layer mix.
2. Adjust EQ and Modules.
3. Click **"Start Capture"** in the **File Transport** panel.
4. The system will record the processed audio in real-time.
5. Click **"Stop Recording"** to finalize the file.
6. Download your master track via the "consentus_master_dump.webm" link.
